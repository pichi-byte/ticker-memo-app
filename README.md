## 🧠 Scoring System (Important)

The scoring framework is **custom-built**, not derived from any universal finance model.

### Quality Score
Measures business strength:
- Profitability
- Margins
- Stability

### Growth Score
Measures expansion potential:
- Revenue growth
- Earnings growth
- Market opportunity

### Risk Score
Measures uncertainty:
- Volatility
- Financial leverage
- Business concentration

Scores are heuristic and designed for demonstration purposes only.
